See inside your own head

Point your webcam at your face. The skull, brain, jaw and eyes follow you in real time — open your mouth and the jaw opens, hold up a hand and its bones appear.

You seem to be inside an app's built-in browser, which often blocks the camera. If “Start camera” doesn't work, open this page in Chrome or Safari.

Video is processed on your device and never uploaded. Tracking models are downloaded once from Google's MediaPipe CDN.

Anatomy Live

An open, dark-themed 3D human anatomy explorer that runs entirely in your browser — no install, no account, no server.

3D Atlas

More than 130 named structures across skin, muscles, skeleton, organs, vessels and nerves, in male and female reference bodies. Toggle systems, search, isolate, explode, and cut sections through the body.

Live Scan

Your webcam is tracked with MediaPipe (face mesh, blendshapes and hands). A skull, brain, jaw, eyes and hand skeleton are drawn on top of you and follow your movement.

Private by design

Camera frames never leave your computer. Everything — tracking and rendering — happens locally with WebGL and WebAssembly.
